# UauGo Privacy Policy

Version 1.14, of 10/21/2026. This is an English translation provided for convenience; in case of any divergence, the Portuguese version prevails.

This Policy explains which personal data the **UauGo** app ("App") processes, for what purpose, with whom it is shared, how long it is kept and how you exercise your rights, under the **Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018, "LGPD")**.

## 1. Who is responsible

- **Controller:** Ricardo Reis, individual, under the HelpAITech brand.
- **Data Protection Officer (privacy contact):** Ricardo Reis — **helpaitech.ricardo@gmail.com**.

## 2. The App's principle: as little as possible on the server

What can stay on your phone stays on your phone: **trip history, settings, phone number, expenses and speed camera and speed limit data**. The server keeps only what must be shared live with other people (ongoing expedition, community alerts and club).

## 3. Data processed and purposes

**Account identification**
- Identifier generated by Firebase (anonymous sign-in) and, if you sign in with Google or email, your **name and email**. Purpose: identify your account, prevent abuse and allow access recovery. Legal basis: performance of contract (art. 7, V).
- Record of your **acceptance of the Terms** and of your **permissions** (version, date and time). Purpose: prove the acceptance. Legal basis: compliance with legal obligation and regular exercise of rights (art. 7, II and VI).

**Location (GPS)**
- **Navigating alone or in "Just drive":** your location is used **only on the phone** (route, warnings, speedometer). It is **not sent to our server**. To calculate routes and search places, the necessary points (origin, destination, stops and the search text) go through our server, which forwards them to Google **without storing them**; for the weather forecast, the points ahead are sent to Open-Meteo.
- **In an expedition:** your position, speed and time are sent to the **expedition participants** (at most once per minute) while it is open in the App. With the App minimized or the screen off, the position keeps being sent only if you turn on **"Share position in the background"** (Settings → Screen; off by default), and then the persistent "UauGo active" notification appears. Outside an expedition, your position is not sent to the group nor stored on the server. Legal basis: consent (art. 7, I), given on the permissions screen and when joining the expedition.
- **SOS:** your position is sent to the expedition participants.
- **Reported alert:** the alert's position and the identifier of who reported it are visible to users while the alert is valid.

**Profile shown to the group**
- Rider name, brand, model, type and color of the vehicle and the **vehicle photo** (if you upload one). Purpose: show who is who on the expedition map.

**Club (optional, only with your permission)**
- Each club has **admins**, enabled with activation codes provided by HelpAITech. To join, each member uses a **single-use invite** generated by an admin. We keep who the admins are and **who invited each member**, to ensure that only official members take part. Admins can remove members. HelpAITech may pre-register the **name** of known clubs; they only appear in the App after an admin of that club activates access.
- **Home city** rounded to ~1 km, name and club. Other members only see the name, the city and the approximate distance, **never your exact position**.
- **Phone number:** stays **only on your phone** and is sent only when you ask for help or notify someone and check that option.

**Voice commands (optional, off by default)**
- If you turn them on in Settings → Accessibility, the App uses the **microphone** with the map open to recognize commands such as "UauGo, radar" and the answers "sim"/"não". Recognition happens **on the phone itself** (offline): **the audio is not recorded, not stored and never leaves the device**. Only recognized commands become actions (for example, an alert). You can turn it off at any time. Legal basis: consent (art. 7, I).
- **"Vehicle stopped on the shoulder" (automatic):** while the App is in use, when you stay stopped for more than 2 minutes on a highway shoulder, the App publishes a temporary "stopped vehicle" alert at that point for drivers coming behind. The alert **does not show who you are** (neither name nor vehicle); only the point and time are kept. It disappears when you start moving again or within 30 minutes, and you can remove it right away by tapping "Don't warn" on the on-screen notice. Legal basis: protection of the life and physical safety of third parties (art. 7, VII) and legitimate interest in road safety (art. 7, IX).
- **Free spoken requests (AI):** when you ask by voice for something outside the command list (e.g. "a good and cheap restaurant"), the **text** of the request (not the audio) is sent to Google's **Gemini**, through the App's server, only to understand what to search for. It is not used for any other purpose and is not stored by the App.
- **Voice route search (optional, off):** if you turn it on, when you say "UauGo, ir para" the App listens to the destination with **Android speech recognition**. When the phone offers on-device recognition, the audio **does not leave the phone**; if not, the audio of that phrase is sent to **Google**, which turns it into text (subject to Google's privacy policy). The App uses the text only to search for the place and does not store the audio. Legal basis: consent (art. 7, I).

**Usage statistics and error reports (can be turned off)**
- **Anonymous usage statistics** (Google Analytics for Firebase): how many people use the App per day, week and month, how long until they come back, which features are used (plan route, Just drive, create or join an expedition, report an alert, voice command), phone model, Android and App version, and approximate region (city/state, estimated from the internet address). It **does not include** your exact location, name, email, routes or the content of expeditions, and **we do not collect the advertising identifier** nor use this data for advertising.
- **Error reports** (Firebase Crashlytics): when the App crashes or fails, it sends what happened (the part of the program that failed), the phone model and versions, so we can fix it.
- Purpose: understand usage and improve the App. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 7, IX). **You can turn it off** at any time in Settings → "Share usage statistics".

**Notifications**
- The phone's notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Purpose: deliver group SOS and stops and club messages.

**Data that stays only on the phone**
- Trip history (path, statistics, expenses), planned trips, settings, vehicle consumption, phone number, **license plate** (used only to warn about the São Paulo plate restriction) and **emergency contact** (name and WhatsApp, used only when you tap SOS). **We have no access to them.** Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

## 4. Who we share with

- **Other users**, only as described above (participants in your expedition, members of your club and public alerts).
- **Processors (service providers):** Google LLC (Firebase: database, files, sign-in, notifications, functions, usage statistics and error reports; Google Maps, Directions and Places) and Open-Meteo (weather forecast, coordinates only, no identification).
- **Authorities**, when there is a legal obligation or court order.
- **We do not sell** personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Where the data is stored

The database, files and server functions are in Google Cloud's **São Paulo (southamerica-east1)** region. Some Google services (such as maps, routes and notifications) may process data on servers outside Brazil, with the safeguards provided in Google's contracts, under art. 33 of the LGPD.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Expedition** (participants, positions, stops, SOS): until the leader ends it or after **30 days without activity**.
- **Community alerts:** for the validity of each type (from **1 hour to 7 days**), then deleted automatically.
- **Club notices:** **180 days**.
- **Club invites:** until they expire (**7 days**), then deleted automatically.
- **Account, permissions and acceptance of the Terms:** while the account exists.
- **Usage statistics:** event details for **2 months**; after that only totals remain (identifying no one).
- **Error reports:** **90 days**.
- **Daily count of route and place queries** (only the number, to limit abuse): replaced every day.
- **Data on the phone:** until you delete it or uninstall the App.

## 7. Your rights (art. 18 of the LGPD)

You may: confirm whether we process your data; access, correct or update your data; request anonymization, blocking or deletion; request portability; know who we share it with; **revoke consent** at any time; and object to processing.

- **In the App:** "Privacy and permissions" lets you change permissions and **delete all your data** (server and phone) immediately.
- **By email:** helpaitech.ricardo@gmail.com. We reply within **15 days**.
- You may also file a complaint with the Brazilian **National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)**.

## 8. Security

We use encrypted connections, database access rules (each person only reads what they are allowed to), app verification (App Check) and automatic deletion of old data. No system is 100% secure: in case of a relevant incident, we will notify those affected and the ANPD, as required by law.

## 9. How to delete your data

In the App: **Privacy and permissions → Delete all my data**. This deletes your account, your participation in expeditions, your club registration and the data on the phone. Alerts you reported are no longer associated with you. If you no longer have the App, request it by email at helpaitech.ricardo@gmail.com.

## 10. Minimum age

The App is intended for people aged **18 or older**. We do not knowingly process data of children or adolescents.

## 11. Changes

We may update this Policy. Relevant changes will be announced in the App and, when necessary, we will ask for new consent.
